Panel Descriptions (2 panels, layout 1×2)

Panel (a) LOW

Panel (a) shows a triangular ternary composition diagram for deep eutectic solvent (DES) mixtures involving choline chloride, with vertices labeled for the three components. A shaded region indicates the eutectic composition range. No quantitative axes with regular tick marks are present; the diagram is a qualitative phase-space schematic that cannot be encoded as a simple data table.

Panel (b) MEDIUM

Panel (b) displays a temperature (°C) vs. concentration (wt%) binary phase diagram for a DES system, showing three regions: Solid (lower-left), Liquid (upper region), and the two-phase Solid–Liquid boundary curve. Annotated points include T_melt(A), T_melt(B), T_eutectic, and the eutectic composition. The diagram illustrates the thermodynamic basis for DES formation and cannot be fully digitised without higher-resolution data.
